# 0.2.2 — 2026-08-12

`chembl_get_drug_info` and `chembl_dataframe_query` close three gaps where a partial or capped result could read as complete.

## Fixed

- **`chembl_get_drug_info`** silently capped `mechanisms` and `indications` at 100 rows each with no way to tell a complete list from a truncated one ([#6](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/6)). `getMechanisms`/`getIndications` now request `this.maxPageSize` (was `limit: 100`) and read `page_meta.total_count`; new `mechanisms_status`/`indications_status` (`complete`\|`truncated`\|`failed`) and nullable `*_total_count` fields disclose it.
- **`chembl_get_drug_info`** collapsed a rejected mechanism or indication request into an indistinguishable empty array, and its "research compound" notice could misattribute the failure to the molecule ([#11](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/11)). A failed list now reports `status: "failed"` with a `null` count, and the notice only claims absent pharmacology when both lists came back `complete` and empty.
- **`chembl_dataframe_query`**'s `format()` silently dropped rows past a hardcoded 50-row cut in `content[]`, even though `structuredContent.rows` carried the full result ([#10](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/10)). Replaced with a 40,000-character render budget disclosed via the new `rendered_rows` field (independent of the existing `truncated` canvas-row-cap field), and struct/list cells now render as JSON in `content[]` instead of `[object Object]`.
