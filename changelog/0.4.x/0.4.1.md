# 0.4.1 — 2026-10-24

## Added

- **`include_alternate_forms`** on `chembl_get_bioactivities` reads the molecule's whole family: its parent and every salt and alternate form filed under it. Without `molecule_chembl_id` it fails `alternate_forms_without_molecule` ([#37](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/37)).
- **`parent_chembl_id`** on `chembl_search_molecules` rows and the `chembl://molecule/{chemblId}` resource names the family parent; a compound with no recorded hierarchy names itself ([#37](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/37)).
- **`variant`** filter on `chembl_get_bioactivities`: `wild_type`, or a recorded mutation matched whole and case-insensitively (`L858R,T790M`; `T790M,L858R` matches nothing) ([#39](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/39)).
- **Row fields** on `chembl_get_bioactivities` and both canvas tables: `activity_comment`, `data_validity_comment` ([#38](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/38)); `assay_variant_mutation`, `assay_variant_accession`, `document_chembl_id`, `document_year` ([#39](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/39)).
- **`chembl_get_drug_info` fields:** `parent_molecule_chembl_id`, `indications_rows_fetched`, `mechanisms_failure` and `indications_failure` (`timeout` or `upstream_error`), `molecule_chembl_id` on each mechanism, and `mesh_id`, `efo_id`, `forms` on each indication ([#36](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/36)).
- **`totalCountCapped`** on `chembl_search_molecules` marks a total at ChEMBL's name (10,000) or substructure (30,000) search ceiling: at least that many compounds match and none past it can be reached ([#42](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/42)).

## Changed

- **The DataCanvas is on by default.** An unset `CANVAS_PROVIDER_TYPE` now means `duckdb`, and `none` disables it; the `.mcpb` bundle pins `none`. Where the variable is unset, a non-cursor `chembl_get_bioactivities` call now spills and stages instead of paging inline, and at the tenant canvas cap it can fail with the retryable `canvas_capacity_exhausted`.
- **A malformed `assay_chembl_id` fails `-32602` before any request,** where it returned `not_found`. This is an error-code change ([#41](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/41)).
- **`limit` above a tool's maximum is served at the maximum** (100 on the search tools, 1,000 on `chembl_get_bioactivities`) with a notice; the schema now rejects only above 10,000 ([#41](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/41)).
- **Activity pages request only the fields the server reads** through ChEMBL's `only=` selection ([#34](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/34)).
- **The inline-versus-spill line** in `chembl_get_bioactivities` allows 800 characters per row (was 600) for the wider rows, and a spilled call's inline preview holds the first `limit` rows ([#39](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/39)).
- **`chembl_get_drug_info` `indications_total_count`** counts distinct indications when the list is `complete`; when `truncated` it stays ChEMBL's row total ([#36](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/36)).

## Fixed

- **`chembl_get_drug_info` reads a drug's family by parent,** so a parent ID or a salt returns the mechanisms and approved indications ChEMBL records on its other forms. Indications merge per (`mesh_id`, `efo_id`) at the highest phase, each form listed at its own ([#36](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/36)).
- **Inputs 0.4.0 answered with an empty success are normalized or refused** ([#41](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/41)):
  - Accepted in any case with surrounding whitespace: `molecule_chembl_id`, `target_chembl_id`, `assay_chembl_id`, and both resources' `chemblId`; a molecule reference that is a standard InChIKey is uppercased too. `accession` and `target_type` are uppercased, and `assay_type` takes a code or ChEMBL's name for it (`binding`, `ADMET`).
  - Refused `-32602` before any request: a `molecule_chembl_id` that is no ChEMBL ID on `chembl_get_bioactivities` (`not_a_chembl_id`, except under `include_alternate_forms`), a `target_chembl_id` that is no ChEMBL ID, and an `assay_type` outside `B`, `F`, `A`, `T`, `P`, `U` and their names.
  - A `standard_type` that matches nothing keeps its exact match, and the notice names the spelling ChEMBL records when only the case differs (`ic50` → `IC50`).
- **An unparseable similarity or substructure SMILES fails `invalid_structure`** (`-32007`) on the first attempt, where it retried four times and reported `upstream_unavailable` ([#43](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/43)).
- **A cursor at a search ceiling** says the ceiling ended the walk, not that the walk is complete ([#42](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/42)).
- **ChEMBL text renders inert in `content[]`.** SMILES such as `C[C@H](N)C(=O)O` no longer parse as links, and assay descriptions, names, and comments keep their brackets, asterisks, and tags as text; `structuredContent` is unchanged ([#40](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/40), [#44](https://github.com/cyanheads/chembl-mcp-server/issues/44)).
